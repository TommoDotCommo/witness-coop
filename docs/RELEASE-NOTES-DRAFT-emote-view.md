# Draft release notes: third-person view while emoting

- **See yourself emote.** When you play an emote (hold `H`), the view moves back behind you, a little above, and you
  see your own adventurer do it; it moves back to first person when the emote ends, when you move or look away, when you click (a puzzle) or
  open a menu. Walls are respected: the camera only goes as far as the room behind you allows, and stays in first person
  when you stand against a wall. The other players see nothing different. Setting: **Third-person view when emoting** (launcher, Co-op
  page; `emote_third_person=0` in `witness_coop.cfg` turns it off).
- Your partner picture (F9 on the other side) shows what you see, so while you emote it shows the third-person view.
