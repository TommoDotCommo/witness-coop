# Draft release notes: third-person view while emoting

- **See yourself emote.** When you play an emote (hold `H`), the view swings round to the front of you, a little above, and you
  see your own adventurer do it; it swings back when the emote ends, when you move or look away, when you click (a puzzle) or
  open a menu. Walls are respected: the camera only goes as far as the room in front of you allows, and stays in first person
  when there is no room. The other players see nothing different. Setting: **Third-person view when emoting** (launcher, Co-op
  page; `emote_third_person=0` in `witness_coop.cfg` turns it off).
- Your partner picture (F9 on the other side) shows what you see, so while you emote it shows the third-person view.
