# Draft release notes: the launcher in the shared shape, pads and the Steam Deck

## The launcher

- **Four tabs: Play, Saves, Settings, About** (it had nine). Play has your name, *Host a game*, *Join a friend* and *Play solo*, and one button that says
  what it does (*Open lobby*, *Join*, *Play solo*); your last choice is remembered. Hosting or joining turns Play into the **Lobby**: players, chat, and
  the invite with *What to play* side by side. The host's button says whom it is waiting for; *Leave* is one click.
- **Settings are saved as you change them**; the *Save settings* button is gone. Sections in the order every co-op mod uses (Co-op rules, Players and
  interface, Voice and sound, Controls, Display, Network, Launcher), each with *Reset section*. Volumes, ranges, the frame limit, field of view and brightness
  are sliders with units; keys are set by pressing them (F1 and keys another action uses are refused, with the reason).
- **The player number is gone**: the lobby gives every player theirs. A direct-play list without a lobby stays under Network.
- **About** has the version and updates, the quick guide, *Send diagnostics* (it was in the bottom bar), links and credits. An update shows as a banner on Play.
- **Pads work in the launcher** (Xbox pads and the Steam Deck): stick or D-pad, A, B, LB/RB for tabs, Start for the main button, X and Y as the footer says.
- **The window is resizable**, opens at 1280x800 (or fills a smaller screen) and fills the screen on a Steam Deck.

## Play in Steam opens the launcher

Pressing Play in Steam now opens the co-op launcher, also in Steam Deck Game Mode: no non-Steam shortcut. The game the launcher starts stays part of
the same Steam launch. To start the game directly from Steam, untick *Show the launcher at start* (Settings, Launcher); hold Shift while the game starts to
get the launcher back.
