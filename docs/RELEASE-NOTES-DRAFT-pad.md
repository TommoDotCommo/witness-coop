# Draft release-note lines: co-op from a controller in the game (not tagged, not published)

- **Co-op on the pad's View button** (Back on older pads; the game never uses it). Tap View to ping what you aim at; hold it for the player list
  (right stick and RT or A: mute, volume, watch, leave). While View is held: D-pad up opens the emote wheel (the right stick picks, letting go of View plays,
  LB turns the page), D-pad down opens chat, D-pad left / right watch the previous / next player, RB opens quick chat, Y shows the quick guide.
  While View is held the game gets nothing from the pad, so you do not walk or turn by accident. Settings, Controls: "Co-op buttons on the pad (View)" turns it off.
- **Quick chat**: Ready, Wait for me, Over here, Come look at this, Thanks (the same phrases as the MDK2 co-op mod). The line goes to everyone like a typed one.
- **Chat without a keyboard.** In Steam's Big Picture or on a Steam Deck, chat opens Steam's floating keyboard; anywhere else a keyboard on screen
  (D-pad or left stick to move, A types, Y space, B deletes or closes, Menu sends).
- **Voice on a pad.** Voice chat has a new default, Automatic: push to talk after keyboard input, open mic (with the voice gate) after pad input.
  "Push to talk on the pad" (Voice and sound) makes a stick click push to talk instead; none by default, because the game's own hints use both stick clicks.
  A mode you chose before (Off, Open mic, Push to talk) stays.
- **Hints follow your hands.** The player panel, the emote wheel, the chat box, the microphone line and the quick guide show pad buttons after pad input
  and keys after keyboard input, switching at once. The quick guide and the launcher's Controls page list the pad's co-op buttons from one table.
- Drawing stays on the keyboard (hold X): it aims with the camera, and every free pad chord would take the right stick from the game while you draw.
- **Steam controller layout** "The Witness Co-op (pad layer)" (Steam Deck and any pad through Steam Input): presents an Xbox gamepad (the game's own Steam
  config presents only keyboard and mouse), Deck L4 = View (ping, player list), R4 = push to talk. See "Steam Deck and controllers" in the README.
